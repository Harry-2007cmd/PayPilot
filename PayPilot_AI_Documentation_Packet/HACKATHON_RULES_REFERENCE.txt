# Context File: PayPal AI Hackathon Official Rules

## 1. Overview & Key Dates
- **Name:** PayPal AI Hackathon
- **Sponsor:** PayPal Inc. (San Jose, CA)
- **Administrator:** Devpost, Inc. (New York, NY)
- **Website:** [paypalaihackathon.devpost.com](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com)
- **Submission Period:** October 1, 2026 (9:15 am PT) – November 12, 2026 (12:00 pm PT)
- **Judging Period:** December 1, 2026 (2:00 pm PT) – December 15, 2026 (8:00 am PT)
- **Winners Announced:** On or around December 21, 2026 (2:00 pm PT)

---

## 2. Eligibility
### **Who CAN Enter:**
- Individuals at least the age of majority in their residence jurisdiction.
- Teams of eligible individuals.
- Organizations (corporations, nonprofits, LLCs, partnerships, etc.) existing/incorporated at entry.
- *Note:* Individuals can join multiple teams/organizations and also enter individually. Teams/Organizations must appoint an authorized "Representative."

### **Who CANNOT Enter:**
- Residents/Organizations domiciled in restricted regions: Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and U.S. OFAC comprehensively sanctioned countries.
- Promotion Entities (Sponsor/Administrator) and their employees, agents, immediate family members (spouse, children, parents, siblings), or household members.
- Judges or companies/individuals employing a Judge.
- Affiliates or parent companies of promotion entities.
- Anyone whose participation creates a real or apparent conflict of interest.

---

## 3. Project Requirements
- **Core Concept:** Build or update an application integrating the **PayPal developer platform** (using the free sandbox environment via `https://developer.paypal.com/`) and an **AI tool, model, or platform** of choice.
- **PayPal Services Included:** Venmo, Braintree, Xoom, Honey, Zettle, Paidy, Hyperwallet, Simility, Swift Financial, etc.
- **Optional AI Sponsor Tools Provided:** AG Grid, APIMatic, Astropods, Bryntum, Channel3, Elastic, KERNEL, Postman, Render, Zapier (using these is optional; any AI tool works as long as PayPal integration is central).
- **Project State:** Must be newly created OR significantly updated after the Hackathon start date.
- **Functionality & Platform:** Must run successfully on its intended platform, function as depicted in videos/descriptions, and use authorized third-party APIs/SDKs/licenses.

---

## 4. Submission Requirements
Submissions must be made on Devpost and include:
1. **Text Description:** Detailed explanation of features and functionality.
2. **Working Demo:** Accessible functional demo (either via clear setup/run instructions in a code repository or a hosted demo URL. Static prototypes/mocks are disqualified).
3. **Public Code Repository:** Public GitHub, Bitbucket, or GitLab repository containing source code, assets, instructions, and an open-source license file visible in the "About" section. Include test account credentials or sandbox login details if private/restricted.
4. **Demonstration Video:** 
   - Under 3 minutes.
   - Showing the project functioning on its built device.
   - Publicly uploaded to YouTube with the link provided in the form.
   - No unauthorized third-party trademarks/copyrighted music.
5. **Language:** Must be in English or accompanied by an English translation.

---

## 5. Judging Criteria & Process
Evaluated in two stages:
- **Stage One (Pass/Fail):** Baseline viability check for theme fit and API/SDK usage.
- **Stage Two (Equally Weighted Criteria):**
  - **Technological Implementation:** Thoroughness and skill using PayPal Developer Platform and AI tools; genuine non-trivial effort.
  - **Design:** Complete, coherent product experience beyond a mere technical proof of concept.
  - **Potential Impact:** Credible, specific case for solving a real problem for a real audience.
  - **Innovation/Idea:** Creativity, novelty, and differentiation from existing concepts.
  - **Presentation:** End-to-end working video demonstration, clear pitch of problem/solution/target audience.
