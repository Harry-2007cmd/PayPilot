# 11 — Demo Script (Under 3 Minutes)

## Preconditions
- App deployed or running reliably.
- PayPal Sandbox buyer/business accounts configured.
- One clean Sandbox checkout ready to demonstrate.
- A clearly labelled reproducible investigation scenario.
- AI API configured; fallback behaviour tested.
- No real credentials visible on screen.

## Script (target 2:40)
**0:00–0:20 — Problem**
“Merchants can spend too much time manually comparing orders, payment statuses, and event records. PayPilot AI turns that investigation into a guided, evidence-backed workflow.”

**0:20–0:55 — PayPal integration**
Show checkout, approve with Sandbox buyer, capture server-side, and open the persisted payment record. Point out provider IDs and Sandbox label.

**0:55–1:20 — Investigation queue**
Open a labelled issue such as a local order/payment status mismatch. Show the order, payment, event timeline, and provenance.

**1:20–2:00 — AI investigation**
Ask: “Investigate this order/payment mismatch. What is verified, what is uncertain, and what should I do next?” Show the agent invoking tools and presenting evidence-linked findings.

**2:00–2:25 — Safe resolution**
Show a pending action. If an eligible Sandbox refund is configured and tested, approve it and show the provider-confirmed result. Otherwise show rejection/review workflow and explain the refund action is gated by eligibility.

**2:25–2:40 — Close**
“PayPilot AI combines PayPal Sandbox payments with controlled AI investigation, deterministic checks, and human-approved actions. It explains what the records show without letting AI independently move money.”

## Video quality checklist
- Public YouTube link.
- Under three minutes.
- English narration or English translation.
- Actual application, not only slides/mockups.
- No secrets, personal data, copyrighted music, or unauthorized marks.
